Mobile Accessories Guide

🔌 Choosing a charging cable

Match the connector to your phone (USB-C, Lightning or Micro USB). Pick a length that suits you, 1 to 1.5 m for most people. A thicker, braided cable lasts longer, and fast charging needs a cable rated for it.

🔌 Charger compatibility

Check your phone’s charging power in watts. A charger with higher wattage is safe because the phone only takes what it needs; a lower one just charges more slowly. Always use a good cable with it.

⚡ Fast charging basics

The charger, the cable and the phone must all support the same fast-charging standard (such as USB Power Delivery or Quick Charge). Charging is fastest up to about half full, then slows down to protect the battery.

🔋 Power bank selection

Capacity is shown in mAh. Some power is lost in conversion, so expect roughly 60–70% usable: a 10000mAh bank gives about two phone charges, 20000mAh about four. Also check the output watts and the ports.

🔁 USB-C vs Lightning

USB-C is reversible and used by most Android phones and by iPhone 15 and newer. Lightning is found on older iPhones. Micro USB is an older Android connector. Check your phone’s port before buying.

✅ Spotting suitable accessories

Check that the product lists your phone’s connector and power rating, buy from a trusted shop, and avoid anything that gets very hot. Ask us on WhatsApp if you’re not sure and we’ll help you choose.
